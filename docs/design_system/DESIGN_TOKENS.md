## 3) Radius Tokens
- `radius-sm`: `8`
- `radius-md`: `12`
- `radius-lg`: `16`
- `radius-xl`: `20`
- `radius-xxl`: `24`
- `radius-pill`: `999`

### Usage
- chips: `radius-pill`
- buttons: `16–20`
- cards: `20`
- bottom sheets: `24`

---

## 4) Elevation Tokens
- `elevation-0`: no shadow
- `elevation-1`: subtle surface separation
- `elevation-2`: primary cards
- `elevation-3`: important floating surfaces

### Rule
Keep elevation soft. The product should feel calm, not glossy.

---

## 9) Layout Rules

### Senior screens
- fewer sections
- larger CTA
- bigger spacing
- less density

### Caregiver screens
- more summary blocks
- clearer status hierarchy
- still avoid overload
