## What this app is useful for

- caregiver dashboard thinking
- daily tasks and activity structure
- support and care coordination
- team / family management
- calendar and schedule ideas
- setup / onboarding ideas for multi-step care flows

---

## What is good here

- very relevant caregiver-side structure
- task and activity management is clear
- team concept is useful for future family linking
- calendar view is helpful for daily care planning
- setup flow looks structured
- support screens show good service/help patterns

---

## What is less useful for Tamnni V1

- too many setup steps for an early simple release
- rewards / gamified parts are not needed now
- special-needs-specific flows do not map directly to elderly reassurance
- some task-heavy screens are broader than Tamnni MVP needs

---

## What Tamnni can learn from this

### For Senior Home
- keep support/help action visible
- keep repeated daily actions simple and structured
- use setup screens carefully and keep them short

### For Caregiver Home
- activity manager ideas are very useful
- task summary sections can inspire a daily status dashboard
- caregiver calendar is useful for future scheduling
- team/family screens are useful for linked caregivers later

### For Alerts / Follow-up
- support-oriented screen language
- task follow-up mindset
- coordinated care thinking

---

## What Tamnni should avoid

- making the first version task-heavy
- adding too many setup screens
- introducing rewards/gamification in V1
- turning the app into a full care operations platform too early

---

## Decision for Tamnni

### Keep / borrow in spirit
- caregiver dashboard structure
- activity/task grouping
- team/family coordination concept
- calendar mindset for future versions
- support/help patterns

### Do not copy directly
- special-needs-specific workflows
- reward mechanics
- large multi-step setup flows
- overly operational care-team complexity in V1

---

## Final takeaway

MY24 is a very useful reference for the **caregiver side** of Tamnni.
Its biggest value is not medication UI, but:
- caregiver task structure
- support flow ideas
- team coordination
- calendar / activity management

Best use for Tamnni:
borrow its caregiver organization ideas, but keep the first version much simpler.
