- Navy: `#0F2144`
- Soft Teal: `#6FD1C6`

## Supporting Brand Colors
- Teal Mist: `#DDF4F2`
- Warm White: `#F9FBFD`
- Text Dark: `#1A2333`
- Text Muted: `#667085`

## Semantic Colors
- Success: `#39A96B`
- Warning: `#F4B740`
- Error: `#D95C5C`
- Info: `#3B82F6`
- Outline: `#D0D7E2`

---

## Brand Usage Guidance

### Navy
Use for:
- primary buttons
- main headings
- trusted status emphasis
- brand anchor color

### Soft Teal
Use for:
- supportive highlights
- medication accents
- secondary emphasis
- soft reassuring UI moments

### Teal Mist
Use for:
- soft containers
- medication cards
- background accents

### Warm White
Use for:
- background
- large readable surfaces

---

## Important Rule
Do not use the logo colors blindly across every UI element.
The UI must stay softer than the logo itself.
