## What is good here

- clear medication timeline by time
- strong reminder concept
- family notification concept is very relevant
- easy-to-scan cards
- medication detail pages feel organized
- progress / adherence screen gives confidence
- adding meds flow looks simple and guided

---

## What is less useful for Tamnni V1

- progress analytics are more advanced than needed
- medication interactions can be postponed after MVP
- too many tabs for the senior role may be unnecessary
- some screens feel a bit dense for an elderly-first app

---

## What Tamnni can learn from this

### For Senior Home
- next medication card
- clear daily medication grouping by time
- easy reminder actions
- calm, readable card layout

### For Caregiver Home
- family notification concept
- status summary idea
- reassurance through visible history / progress

### For Medication Feature
- timeline layout
- medication details page
- add medication/import flow
- taken / snooze / skip actions

---

## What Tamnni should avoid

- making the home screen too crowded
- adding too many secondary tabs in V1
- making the interface feel too clinical
- showing too much data to the senior user at once

---

## Decision for Tamnni

### Keep / borrow in spirit
- medication cards grouped by time
- family alert concept
- large clear medication actions
- clean blue/white healthcare structure

### Do not copy directly
- exact tab layout
- dense progress views
- complex medication interaction pages for V1
